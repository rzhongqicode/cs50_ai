# Analysis

## Layer 4, Head 6

 tokens appear to pay attention to the tokens that precede it;

Example Sentences:
- China is the [MASK] country in the world.
- It is going to [MASK] tomorrow.

## Layer 8, Head 11

Articles like 'a' and 'the' pay attention to the noun that follows them

Example Sentences:
- I love the [MASK].
- [MASK] wrote a letter.
